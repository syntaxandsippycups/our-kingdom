The complete development brief and authoritative story bible are included beside this checklist. Historical facts remain in `public/story-data/story.json`; original vows and the anniversary declaration are kept verbatim there. Game-authored dialogue is explicitly labeled fictional.

| Brief requirement | Game implementation |
| --- | --- |

Most optional memories now use short character-led scenes with Aaron choosing his own responses. Game-written dialogue remains labeled as imagined in the story data and is never presented as historical quotation. The fourth child is described only as expected, consistent with the supplied story timeline. The intended campaign pace is 25–40 minutes; it remains a design target pending a full browser playtest.
